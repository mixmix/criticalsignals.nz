The purpose of this seminar is to share experiences and reflections on co-production of "law from below" with Indigenous and Local Communities. Dr Francesca Marzatico (School of Surveying - University of Otago) and Dr.  Giuseppe Micciarelli, University of Naples Federico II, Naples (UNINA) will discuss and share their experiences.
