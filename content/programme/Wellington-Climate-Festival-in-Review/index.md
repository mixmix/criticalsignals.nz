The Pōneke Climate festival is a three-week community-led programme of events, activations, and creative climate solutions designed to bring together organisations, communities, and individuals to accelerate climate action across Wellington.

Due to Government funding cuts, the Wellington Climate Festival has dropped from our city's schedule. But do they know it's impact on Wellington? Kate Kirkwood joins us at 113, to share their findings from the Climate Festival 2026. What impact is it having in Wellington, and what are people most concerned with? Who is showing up to make change, and who is funding it?
