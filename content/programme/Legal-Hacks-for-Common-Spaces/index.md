**Doors open 5.00pm for drinks, nibbles and kōrero. Talk starts at 6.00pm.**

Communities should be able to use empty spaces for collective good. That means community gardens instead of an empty parking lot, or a tool library in that shed that hasn't been used for 5 years. This is what "Urban Commoning" looks like.

In the second part of of our Urban Commoning series, we will be learning about how we can re-write the laws to empower community to use the empty spaces around us. Giuseppe Micciarelli is flying in from Italy, where he was an instrumental lawmaker who paved the way for recoginising the commons officially in Italian law.

Last time you brought a photo of an urban space, green area, or building that has  
been abandoned or fallen into disuse—perhaps one whose history you  
already know or would like to investigate further. This time, we will delve further into a few of the spaces we discovered last time, and explore the changes we can ask our politicians to make, so that the people who live in the neighbourhood, can benefit from the neighbourhood.

There will be nibbles, discussion, and the chance to make a change in the city you live in.
