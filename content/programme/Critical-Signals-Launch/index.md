You are invited to the launch of Critical Signals, a three month public laboratory of artists, activists, scientists, makers and the public to explore crises, commons, and community in Wellington City.

2026 marks a new partnership with the European National Institutes of Culture to support the hosting of talks, panels, exhibits and workshops to stimulate discourse, curate connection and incubate action.

Gain an understanding of the program, meet Critical Signals contributors, and celebrate the partnership between EUNIC and Critical Signals.
