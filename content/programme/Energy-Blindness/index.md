**How do we redesign the economic system around the wellbeing of our people and te taiao?**

Modern economics has a dangerous blind spot: it treats energy as just another input, when in reality energy is the foundation of everything we call “the economy”.

Nate Hagens describes “energy blindness” as our cultural and economic failure to recognise energy as the foundational input that makes modern life possible. This blindness sits at the heart of many of today’s crises: climate disruption, ecological overshoot, inequality, cost-of-living pressures, and political short-termism.

In this presentation, WEAll Aotearoa explores how a wellbeing economy can help us see more clearly. Rather than treating GDP growth as the ultimate goal, a wellbeing economy asks what the economy is for: meeting the needs of people and te taiao, now and for future generations.

By connecting energy reality with economic redesign, we can begin to build systems that are fairer, more resilient, and grounded within planetary boundaries.
