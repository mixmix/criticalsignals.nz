Join Photographer Andy Spain and Architect Tina Williams for a walking tour
through the streets of Wellington exploring the inspirations and histories of
the buildings which feature in the GHOST FORESTS exhibition.

**The tour starts at the Old Government Buildings, 55 Lambton Quay**, and
finishes back at Critical Signals, 113 Taranaki Street.

**Accessibility:** the tour is on flat public footpaths, with a light incline
along the terrace. Tours are limited to approximately 15 people. Booking
essential.

**Weather:** we will be in contact with ticket holders the day before if the
forecast looks unfavourable. Rain-check day is 3 October.

<img class="divider" src="/images/design/divider1.webp" alt="">

**Ghost Forests** is a photographic and moving image project that explores the
ecological histories hidden within New Zealand's architecture and urban
environment. It explores the relationship between buildings like the
Wellington's Old Government Building — one of the world's largest surviving
timber (Kauri) buildings—and the forests that supplied its construction
material. The project seeks to reconnect architecture with its environmental
origins.
The exhibition is open for 2 weeks - [see details here](/programme/andy-spain-ghost-forests-exhibition/).
