**Rebel Tech Alliance was formed in early 2025 by Patrick Leavy and a friend to stop Big Tech spying on people and monetising personal data.** They do that by educating about the dangers of Big Tech and showing people what the ethical alternatives are. As a not-for-profit they offer for free the Big Tech Walkout Programme, which allows people to move to products that don't "feed the beast". Patrick will speak on the origins of Rebel Tech Alliance, where the project is headed and how we can sidestep Big Tech's creepy world of surveillance and control.
