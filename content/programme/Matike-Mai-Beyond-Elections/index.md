Matike Mai Aotearoa is a Māori-led conversation about what a just and inclusive constitutional future for Aotearoa could look like - one that truly honours Te Tiriti o Waitangi and creates belonging for all people in Aotearoa. Created by Pa Moana Jackson, Prof Margaret Mutu and Dr Veronica Tawhai, Matike Mai looks at transforming our constitution as the ultimate systems change in Aotearoa, rather than focussing on Westminster laws and policies that are subject to change again every three years as governments change. 

Join Erin Matariki Carr at Critical Signals on Weds 21 October 2026, 12pm, to learn more about the Matike Mai Report and how we can join the movement today!
