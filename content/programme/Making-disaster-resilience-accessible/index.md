**Preparation is the key to handing disasters big and small, and disabled people are often left out of disaster preparedness planning.** **This oversight threatens lives.** In this disabled-led workshop, Katie shares how her experiences working in some of the most extreme environments on earth- from the stormy Southern Ocean to managing live events during a global pandemic- has provided a foundation for accessible disaster planning.
