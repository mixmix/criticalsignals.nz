The story of a contemporary hero – Lech Wałęsa. An electrician at the Gdańsk Shipyards, who becomes an unexpected and charismatic leader of Polish dockworkers, looking to form a union. A bold, humourous, and human depiction of this historical moment, this movie shows his path to political maturity, interwoven with the Wałęsas' family life. The film, just as Lech Wałęsa's life, is full of contrasts... Is Lech making the right choices? What is the price he has to pay?

Film generously provided by the Polish embassy, who will also be there on the night to talk to us about the significance of the film.

Screening starts at 6.00pm, Doors open with nibbles and drinks 5.00pm.
