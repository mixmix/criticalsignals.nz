Join us as we gather for five three-hour sessions over ten weeks, to make one small, powerful asset for the Pōneke climate community — a tool or resource that continues to serve the community into the future.

We choose what to build in the first session, then use the next four to make it, test it with real people, and get it ready to use. We have a budget to spend on the build, and want to create a critically useful resource for organisations working on climate change and other critical impact work in our city.

Not required to attend all sessions though being available for most would be beneficial.

In collaboration with two/fiftyseven and the Pōneke Climate Hub.
