<p class="intro">Critical Signals is a collective endeavour, and it runs on contributions of ideas, time, and resources. Here are the ways you can get involved.</p>

<img class="divider" src="/images/design/divider2.webp" alt="">

## Volunteer

Critical Signals is built by the community that shows up for it. Volunteers help host events, welcome guests, and keep things running smoothly across our three months in Wellington.

No prior experience is needed, just a sense of our tikanga and a few hours to spare. Keep an eye on the [programme](/programme/) for upcoming volunteer orientation sessions, or get in touch to register your interest.

<p class="note">Email us at <a href="mailto:contact@criticalsignals.nz">contact@criticalsignals.nz</a> to find out what's needed and when.</p>

## Propose an event

<h3 class="caps">2026 Themes - Democracy and Energy</h3>

#### Stuff that Sustains

The life, culture, energy, and resources that sustain us, and what might be enough.

#### Community Energy

The infrastructure and the people that keep us alive, with the collective will to act together.

#### Unfolding Futures

The multiple futures unfolding before us, and how we prepare in community.

#### Next Step Democracy

What comes next for the ways we work, decide, and build our own worlds together.

#### Politics of Space

How we utilise the power of space and place in ways that enable us all.

### We welcome contributions in any of the following formats:

<div class="focus" style="margin-top: 1rem;">

- Workshops
- Talks
- Skills sharing
- Community connections (one-off or recurring)
- Practical making events
- Artworks and experiences

</div>

<p class="note">We particularly value evidence-based, original material. Where ideas overlap, we will favour workshops that engage participants in more ways than words alone. We endeavour to respond to all proposals promptly.</p>

### Contributing from outside Aotearoa NZ

Our events take place in person in Wellington, and we warmly welcome international contributors. If you are based in Europe or further afield, we can support remote and online participation: talks, workshops, and conversations delivered across time zones, as well as artworks and experiences presented on your behalf. Please tell us in your proposal how you would like to take part, and we will work with you to make it possible.
