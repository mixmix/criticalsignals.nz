Tom Beauchamp is a Licensed Building Practitioner with a diverse background
spanning sustainable construction, project management, design, and the
creative arts. He is the founder of a successful eco-building company
delivering low-carbon, high-performance buildings using natural materials and
hybrid systems. He brings a unique combination of technical building
expertise, creative problem-solving, and collaborative leadership, and is
experienced in managing projects end-to-end, working closely with clients,
designers, and teams to deliver thoughtful, durable, and environmentally
responsible outcomes.
