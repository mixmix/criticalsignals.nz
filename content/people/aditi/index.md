title: "Aditi Tiwari"
date: 2026-10-19
---

Aditi (she/her) is a Master of Professional Business Analysis student at Victoria
University of Wellington, whose research explores digital leadership, AI
governance and Indigenous data sovereignty in AI-enabled public sector systems
through a Te Tiriti o Waitangi lens.

Her work examines the intersections of emerging technologies, power,
accountability and data sovereignty, particularly how decisions about the
collection, ownership and use of data can reinforce existing structural
inequalities.

As a first-generation migrant and community advocate, Aditi brings both
academic and lived perspectives to conversations about digital equity,
representation and the concentration of power in technological systems. She is
particularly interested in how AI governance can uphold Indigenous rights,
strengthen democratic accountability and ensure communities retain meaningful
control over their data and digital futures.
