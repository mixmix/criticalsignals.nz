Dr Mike Joy MSc, PhD (Ecology) is a Senior Research Fellow and Lecturer in
Environmental Science at Victoria University of Wellington. Research includes
ecological modelling, freshwater bioassessment, environmental policy,
biophysical limits to growth and the role of energy in society.

Recipient of numerous awards including the Callaghan Medal for science
communication from the Royal Society of New Zealand 2023.
