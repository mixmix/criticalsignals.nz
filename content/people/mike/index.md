Dr Mike Joy (he/him) is a Senior Research Fellow in the School of Geography, Environment
and Earth Sciences at Te Herenga Waka Victoria University of Wellington, and an
outspoken advocate for environmental protection in New Zealand. His research
includes ecological modelling, freshwater bioassessment, environmental policy
development, biophysical limits to growth, and the role of energy in society.
He has provided expert evidence for high court, environment court and Waitangi
Tribunal cases, and has communicated extensively about the influence of vested
interests on environmental policy.

Mike is the recipient of numerous awards for his research and science
communication, including the Morgan Foundation's inaugural River Voice Award,
the inaugural NZ Universities Critic and Conscience award, an Ecology in Action
award from the NZ Ecological Society, the Charles Fleming Award for
environmental achievement, and the Callaghan Medal for science communication
from the Royal Society of New Zealand.
