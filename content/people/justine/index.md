title: "Justine Prain"
date: 2026-08-11
---

Justine Prain is a New Zealand sustainability and climate action leader
serving as the Head of Commercial and Operations at the Sustainability Trust
in Wellington. She focuses on residential energy transitions,
decarbonisation, and community-scale retrofitting to create warmer,
healthier, and energy-efficient homes.

She spent five years as a Project Manager and Programme Director for
Energiesprong UK and is the Rewiring Aotearoa Community Lead for the
Wairarapa.
