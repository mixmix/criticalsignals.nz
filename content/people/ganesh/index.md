title: "Ganesh Ahirao"
date: 2026-08-11
---

Dr Ganesh Ahirao (aka Ganesh Nana) has over 45 years of professional
experience in economics. He has used this time to fight for fairer outcomes
in our society, and to argue for changes to establish an economic system that
properly serves the communities of Aotearoa, rather than vice-versa.

He currently occupies his time in voluntary roles with several charities
including Treasurer for Kaibosh. He has previously been Chair of the
Productivity Commission Te Kōmihana Whai Hua o Aotearoa, and led BERL — a
NZ-owned consultancy — to be an Accredited Living Wage employer.
