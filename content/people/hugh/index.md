Hugh Tennent FNZIA studied architecture at the University of Auckland,
graduating in 1984, and was made a Fellow of Te Kāhui Whaihanga / the New
Zealand Institute of Architects in 2001. In 2003 he and Ewan Brown founded
[Tennent Brown Architects](https://tennentbrown.co.nz/) in Te Whanganui-a-Tara,
a practice built on equity and social justice that works across ngā motu on
large public and commercial projects.

Hugh is design director for the practice, responsible for the design direction
of all its major projects and recognised for his responsiveness to context and
environment. He is particularly known for work across cultures and for te ao
Māori — Pā Reo at Te Wānanga o Raukawa, Te Kura Whenua for Ngāi Tūhoe at
Waikaremoana, Ngā Mokopuna at Te Herenga Waka — as well as education,
reflective and religious space, and sports buildings. He lectures, tutors and
critiques regularly at the Victoria University of Wellington School of
Architecture.

In 2024 Hugh and Ewan were jointly awarded the NZIA Gold Medal, the Institute's
highest honour, for a body of work spanning 22 years and 75 NZIA awards, and
for leading a practice at the vanguard of radical sustainability and bicultural
collaboration. Tennent Brown was the first Aotearoa practice to obtain a JUST
label for social justice (2018) and the first to achieve B Corp certification
(2022).
