Ewan Brown FNZIA is a director of
[Tennent Brown Architects](https://tennentbrown.co.nz/), the Te
Whanganui-a-Tara practice he founded with Hugh Tennent in 2003. His leadership
has propelled the practice into becoming a sustainability innovator: Tennent
Brown was the first Aotearoa practice to obtain a JUST label for social justice
(2018), a founding member of The Diversity Agenda, and the first architecture
practice in the country to achieve B Corp certification (2022).

Ewan has driven the practice's work under the Living Building Challenge — one
of the world's most demanding sustainability standards — on projects including
Te Wharehou o Waikaremoana, Pā Reo and Te Kāhui Tapuwae / The Living Pā,
building a repository of LBC-compliant material knowledge along the way. In
association with Hugh Tennent, his collaborative efforts push boundaries and
reshape industry standards, guiding projects toward community wellbeing,
environmental harmony and carbon neutrality.

In 2024 Ewan and Hugh were jointly awarded the NZIA Gold Medal, Te Kāhui
Whaihanga's highest honour, recognising 22 years of practice, 75 NZIA awards,
and their role as early adopters and risk takers in the profession's response
to the climate crisis.
