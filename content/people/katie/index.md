Katie is currently working with Arts Access Aotearoa to increase accessibility in the arts to Deaf and disabled people. She blends her lived and professional experience as a disabled person, tall ship sailor, and theatre stage manager to support individuals and organisations to remove barriers through creativity and education. When she’s not passionately encouraging the removal of barriers to the arts, she can be found hiking, climbing, and writing. Originally from the US, Katie has lived in Aotearoa since 2016.
