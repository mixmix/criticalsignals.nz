title: "Katie Querin"
date: 2026-07-30
---

Katie Querin has over a decade of experience working in theatre and arts
accessibility, and in her role leading the Arts For All Network, she supports
individuals and organisations across Aotearoa to increase their
accessibility to Deaf and disabled people. Working with anyone from
independent artists to national arts organisations, Katie facilitates
regular in person and online hui, offers individual advisory sessions and
delivers arts accessibility workshops across the mōtū. Katie blends her
lived experience as a disabled person into her professional mahi.
