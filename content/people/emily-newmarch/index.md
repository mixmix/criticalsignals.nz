Dr Emily Newmarch is Sustainability Lead and Low Carbon Design Specialist at
[Warren and Mahoney](https://wam.studio/), where she works as a translator
between technical sustainability data and the creative opportunities that are
best for a project. She holds a PhD in embodied carbon from Te Herenga Waka
Victoria University of Wellington, and helped develop the practice's *Go Zero*
low carbon design guide.

In 2025 Emily was awarded the overall Supreme Award at the Property Council New
Zealand People Awards in recognition of her impact in translating PhD-level
climate research into practical low-carbon design solutions across sectors from
health to aviation. Selected as the standout achiever across all categories,
and also recipient of the Young Achiever Award, she is redefining what
sustainable design leadership looks like in Aotearoa.

One of her favourite projects, Te Huhi Raupō — the Taranaki Hospital renal unit
— is small but packs a big punch: it is set to be among the first in the world
to achieve Zero Carbon Certification from the International Living Future
Institute.

> Whilst climate change is our biggest threat, it is also our biggest
> opportunity to think differently and innovate towards new ways of designing
> and building.
