date: 2026-08-31
---

Emma is a sound designer, producer and co-founder of DIY electronic music
initiative Tonic. She regularly performs under Ludus and BIRDPARTY, but her
biggest love in sound is field recording, using listening and sonic
documentation to deepen our sense of place. Emma's practice explores
collaboration as its own form of artistic expression, and the potential of
sound to highlight both the beauty in the world and community needs.
