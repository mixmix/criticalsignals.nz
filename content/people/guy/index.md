Guy Shaw is a Registered Architect and Certified Passive House Designer, and
the founder of Energy Architecture NZ. With nearly two decades of Passive
House and retrofit experience across the UK and New Zealand — designing,
modelling, and testing buildings from research huts to high-rise residential
— he brings an unusually broad view of what high-performance buildings can
and should be.

He has a particular interest in the gap between what the building code
aspires to and what its compliance pathways actually deliver — and what that
means for the health of New Zealanders.
