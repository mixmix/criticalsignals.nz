title: "Julia Steinberger"
date: 2026-07-30
---

Professor Julia Steinberger is a professor of societal impacts of climate
change at the University of Lausanne. Steinberger's research examines the
connections between resource use, and societal performance. She is
interested in quantifying the current and historical linkages between
resource use and socioeconomic parameters, and identifying development
pathways to guide the transition to a low carbon society.
