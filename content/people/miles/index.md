Miles Thompson is a machine learning engineer and programmer who has been
training neural networks and building models since [before anybody
cared](https://github.com/utunga/hashmapd). He works at Te Hiku Media, training
and deploying [foundation language models](https://papareo.io) for
Māori/English - and other indigeneous languages under the kaitiakitanga
license. He is also starting up [ChurPrimo](http://churprimo.org/) which is
trying to solve real-time agentic AI governance, and has been interested in
mutual aid, local economic resilience and collaborative finance for a minute.
His dream is to replace all the banks with a small shell script.
