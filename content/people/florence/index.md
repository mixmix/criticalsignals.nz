Florence Hillyer-Brandt graduated from Victoria of University with a Bachelor of Design Innovation, specialising in Game Design. Since then, her career has shifted from games to politics, to events. Working with the Green Party of Aotearoa, and then being the Creative Director of NZ Games Festival, has put in her in contact with a broad range of people, audiences, and events. She still believes strongly in the connective power of games, and the transformative power of meeting and connecting with others in space.
