title: "Mike Anthony"
date: 2026-08-11
---

Mike Anthony has been working on systems change for 25 years, including on
human rights systems in Africa and Asia, and internationally at the UN, as
well as climate and ecological systems breakdown. He founded GoZero in
Wellington in 2021, to accelerate greenhouse gas emissions reductions by
individuals and businesses.

He is now also focussing on sufficiency and community resilience work to
address the wider ecological and economic systems crises.
