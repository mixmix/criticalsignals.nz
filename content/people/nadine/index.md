date: 2026-07-23

Nadine works in the intersection between the arts, science, policy, land reclamation and environmental restoration. She is passionate about all forms of indigenous storytelling\*, in particular supporting other indigenous writers to enter the world of publishing through zines, chapbooks and other independent pathways. She is a solo Māmā and has written extensively for The Spinoff, E-Tangata and other platforms. In 2025, her collection of essays [Slowing the Sun](https://www.bwb.co.nz/books/slowing-the-sun?srsltid=AfmBOorNgXfhWUy3Qr4EOJFD_iCMvJyHvOW_H4K50dz5VBmLTSGRxOvv) was published by BWB.

_\*except A.I._
