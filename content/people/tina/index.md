Tina Williams is a registered architect (NZRAB) based in Te Whanganui-a-Tara
Wellington, and Chairperson of the Wellington branch of
[Te Kāhui Whaihanga / the New Zealand Institute of Architects](https://www.nzia.co.nz/about-us/our-structure/).
In that role she leads the branch committee and the local programme of the
Aotearoa Festival of Architecture.
