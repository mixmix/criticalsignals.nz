title: "William Mason"
date: 2026-09-07
---

William Mason is an Architectural Graduate at McKenzie Higham Architects,
holding a Master of Architecture (Professional) from Te Herenga Waka —
Victoria University of Wellington, where his research focused on bio-based
self-build housing for rural Aotearoa. He co-founded the Bio-based Building
Network (BBBN), advocating for natural materials in the built environment. He
has written on hemp and straw construction for Newsroom, and was a finalist for
the NZGBC Future Thinker of the Year 2026 award.
