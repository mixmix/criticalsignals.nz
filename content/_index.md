<p>Critical Signals is a three month gathering, practicing community resilience in person based in Wellington, Aotearoa New Zealand.</p>

<p>We collaborate with artists, academics, activists, scientists, technologists, makers and the public to explore crises, commons and community, stimulating critical discourse and action through trusted networks.</p>

<div class="theme">
  <p>2026 Themes</p>
  <h3 class="caps">Democracy and Energy</h3>
  <h4>Stuff that Sustains</h4>
  <p>The life, culture, energy, and resources that sustain us, and what might be enough.</p>
  <h4>Community Energy</h4>
  <p>The infrastructure and the people that keep us alive, with the collective will to act together.</p>
  <h4>Unfolding Futures</h4>
  <p>The multiple futures unfolding before us, and how we prepare in community.</p>
  <h4>Next Step Democracy</h4>
  <p>What comes next for the ways we work, decide, and build our own worlds together.</p>
  <h4>Politics of Space</h4>
  <p>How we utilise the power of space and place in ways that enable us all.</p>
