Critical Signals is a three month gathering, practicing community resilience in person based in Wellington, Aotearoa New Zealand, running 1 August – 31 October 2026.

Sign up above to hear updates from the community creating the project, and don’t miss our launch and programme announcements. Or if you’ve got a workshop, talk, artwork, or idea you’d like to bring to the programme, tell us about it via the proposal link.

You can also reach us directly at contact@criticalsignals.nz
