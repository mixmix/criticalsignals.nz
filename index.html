Critical Signals

Critical Signals is a collective endeavour for creative resilience: a gathering place for people who believe communities shape their own futures.

Call for proposals Sign up for news

Critical Signals is a three month gathering, practicing community resilience in person based in Wellington, Aotearoa New Zealand.

We collaborate with artists, academics, activists, scientists, technologists, makers and the public to explore crises, commons and community, stimulating critical discourse and action through trusted networks.

Democracy and Energy

Stuff that Sustains

The life, culture, energy, and resources that sustain us, and what might be enough.

Community Energy

The infrastructure and the people that keep us alive, with the collective will to act together.

Unfolding Futures

The multiple futures unfolding before us, and how we prepare in community.

Next Step Democracy

What comes next for the ways we work, decide, and build our own worlds together.

Politics of Space

How we utilise the power of space and place in ways that enable us all.
