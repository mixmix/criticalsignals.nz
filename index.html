Critical Signals

Critical Signals is a gathering place for people who believe communities shape their own futures.

Critical Signals hosts talks, workshops, and gatherings, where our local community can navigate disruptions in our society, culture, and climate.

We connect artists, academics, activists and makers with the public to explore challenges and solutions, sparking critical discourse and action.

Stuff that Sustains

Unfolding Futures

The multiple futures unfolding before us, and how we prepare in community.

Next Step Democracy

What comes next for the ways we work, decide, and build our own worlds together.

Politics of Space

How we utilise the power of space and place in ways that enable us all.
